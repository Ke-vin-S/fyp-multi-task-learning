     The Four Architectures (Section 5.2)

     Presented as a unified design space, not per-person:

     1. PMR (Task Relation Learning) — Kevin's branch. Precision matrix Ω via
     Cholesky factorization. Regularizer-based (no architecture change at
     inference). Proven in CV by Zhang & Yeung, Gonçalves et al. Already
     implemented.
     2. Low-Rank Parameter Subspace (Low-Rank) — Chehan's branch. W_i =
     U·diag(s_i)·V^T. Shared basis U,V with per-task coefficients s_i. Rank r
     controls sharing degree. Based on Argyriou et al., Ando & Zhang.
     3. Task Clustering via Soft Assignment (Task Clustering) — Induwara's
     branch. K cluster experts combined via learned gating network. α_i =
     softmax(gate(task_embed_i)). Based on Jacob et al., Fifty et al. (TAG), Li
     et al. (Grad-TAG).
     4. Decomposition with Dynamic Splitting (Decomposition) — Pathumi's branch.
      W_i = P + Q_i (shared low-rank + task-specific sparse). Dynamic layer
     duplication when gradient conflict exceeds threshold. Based on Jalali et
     al. (Dirty Model), Recon (ICLR 2023).
