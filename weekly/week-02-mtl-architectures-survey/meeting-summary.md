### Meeting Notes - 2026/07/09

- The team presented the five MTL approaches from the survey paper. The supervisors emphasized that future work should focus on comparing and evaluating these approaches rather than only explaining them.
- A benchmark comparison should be prepared by comparing each selected architecture against the WavCSE baseline in terms of architecture design and implementation complexity.
- Team members should study their assigned research papers, understand the proposed architectures, design modified versions based on the WavCSE model and prepare an implementation plan with estimated development time.
- All members should explore the LibMTL library, study its implementations and categorize its architectures according to the five MTL approaches discussed in the survey.
- Since many MTL architectures are designed for image data the team should investigate how these methods can be adapted for speech embeddings generated by the WavCSE model.
- The team was encouraged to begin implementation work immediately, provide regular progress updates and work towards developing a configurable MTL framework that supports multiple architectures within a shared codebase.
